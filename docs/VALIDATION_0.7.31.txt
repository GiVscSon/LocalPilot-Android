SCOPE AND LIMITS
No physical Android device is connected. Native fixture is an independent
synthetic target in the test APK, not Yandex Market. It validates Android
capture/gesture/verification/STOP; it does not establish real wall position,
score improvement, quest completion, rewards or compatibility of all ten games.
Main APK does not include the synthetic target game.

Bubble geometry is bounded to four ordinary colors and at most one inferred
side-wall reflection. Aim ends at first wall, impact coordinates are separate.
Exact expected field remains mandatory after ACK; unsupported changes pause input.
Guide source: user material, checked against code; external reviews and reward
amounts not independently verified. Six-by-six octopus swap preserved, not
silently converted to a line-path game. Hexa Sort/Kototower remain unsupported.
